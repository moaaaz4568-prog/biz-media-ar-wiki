# T_espresso_dial_01

A double espresso should
pull between 25 and 30 seconds; shorter tastes sour, longer tastes
"bitter and muddy." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_espresso_dial_02

Adjust the grind, not the dose, when the time drifts —
changing the dose to fix timing hides the real problem and teaches new
"baristas the wrong habit." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_espresso_dial_03

Grinder blades wear out every six to eight weeks and the drift they cause
"is slow enough to miss." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_espresso_dial_04

"When shots creep past 32 seconds for a whole morning even after adjustment, the blades are done — order replacements the same day" — sources/S_ops_handbook.md

# T_espresso_dial_05

On rainy days the beans absorb
"moisture and the same setting pulls slower; on dry days, faster." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_espresso_dial_06

The dial is not a set-and-forget number, it is a morning
"instrument reading." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
