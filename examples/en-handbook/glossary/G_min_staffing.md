# G_min_staffing — Minimum Staffing

## Term

Minimum staffing in this handbook is exactly three people on shift: one
on till, one on bar, one floating. It is not a suggestion or an average —
it is the line below which the queue reaches the door in under twenty
minutes, measured in October 2025.

## Synonyms

- three-person rule (staff shorthand)
- floor minimum

## Live example

> A shift needs a minimum of three people: one on till, one on bar, one
"floating." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
## When the meaning breaks

- During a scheduled deep-clean close, two people are acceptable because
  the till is closed and the door is locked — the term applies to
  trading hours only.
