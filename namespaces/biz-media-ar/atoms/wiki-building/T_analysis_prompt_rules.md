# T_analysis_prompt_rules_01

"Never invent facts, numbers, names, prices or claims. Every on-screen word comes from my transcript (shortened is fine, changed meaning is not)." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_02

"Graphics must EXPLAIN what I say (steps, comparisons, numbers, UI, flows). Decoration without meaning is a failure." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_03

"Arabic is sacred: always RTL; never animate Arabic letter by letter (animate per word or per line); never add letter-spacing to Arabic; isolate English terms as LTR" — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_04

"All colors, fonts, sizes, radii, shadows and timings come from tokens in `MOTION.md`. No ad-hoc values." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_05

"Work silently and autonomously. The only question you may ask is the audio-only format question." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_06

"Read the `SKILL.md` of every skill before using it." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_07

"Build a glossary of product/model/command names from the transcript and spell them consistently in English everywhere." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md

# T_analysis_prompt_rules_08

"Cut only at word boundaries from the transcript, snapped to zero-crossings, with 15-20 ms audio crossfades. Never cut inside a word." — sources/S_wkt_prompt.md
