# G_shot_clock — Shot Clock

## Term

The shot clock is the time a double espresso takes to pull, measured
from the pump start to the last drip. In this handbook it is the heart
of quality control: a number, not a metaphor. The target band is 25 to
30 seconds, and every grind adjustment exists to stay inside it.

## Synonyms

- pull time (used in Section 2 interchangeably)
- timing (informal, among staff)

## Live example

> A double espresso should
pull between 25 and 30 seconds; shorter tastes sour, longer tastes
"bitter and muddy." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
## When the meaning breaks

- When the grinder blades are worn, the clock reads slow for mechanical
  reasons no setting fixes — the term then describes the machine's
  health, not the coffee's quality.
