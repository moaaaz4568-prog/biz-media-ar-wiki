# T_opening_01

Espresso machines need forty minutes of warm-up time before they pull a
"shot worth serving." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_opening_02

A machine turned on at 6:15 produces sour
shots until 6:55, and a sour first shot costs you the regular who always
"orders a flat white at 6:45." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
# T_opening_03

"goes back on the truck, no exceptions." — sources/S_ops_handbook.md
